# 21-day AZ-104 study sprint

Target exam: 30 November 2026. Start: 9 November 2026. Confirm the current skills outline in the official study guide.

## Week 1 — foundations (9–15 Nov)
| Day | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Azure architecture, subscriptions, ARM, CLI/PowerShell | Explain tenant/subscription/resource hierarchy |
| 2 | Entra users/groups, RBAC and scopes | Test least-privilege role assignment |
| 3 | Policy, initiatives, locks, tags, budgets | Governance exercise |
| 4 | VNets, address spaces, subnets, IPs | Design and create lab VNet |
| 5 | NSGs, ASGs, routes | Troubleshoot blocked test traffic |
| 6 | Peering, DNS, private/service endpoints, VPN concepts | Compare connectivity options |
| 7 | Storage accounts, redundancy, Blob | Create private test container |

## Week 2 — services (16–22 Nov)
| Day | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | SAS, Entra storage access, Azure Files, AzCopy | Controlled test-file access |
| 9 | VM deployment, sizing, disks, NICs | Deploy/inspect small VM if affordable |
| 10 | VM availability, scale sets, troubleshooting | Explain availability choices |
| 11 | App Service, plans, scaling, slots, TLS | Deploy or document low-cost app |
| 12 | Containers, ACR, ACI, Container Apps | Compare and practise container concepts |
| 13 | Monitor, Activity Log, metrics, KQL | Run and explain a log query |
| 14 | Alerts, action groups, Backup, vaults | Alert and recovery workflow |

## Final preparation (23–29 Nov)
| Day | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Integration and Bicep/ARM | Validate template and review architecture |
| 16 | Identity, networking, storage troubleshooting | Incident records |
| 17 | Compute, monitoring, backup troubleshooting | Incident records |
| 18 | Microsoft practice assessment | Review all wrong/guessed answers |
| 19 | Timed scenarios and weak domains | Ranked revision list |
| 20 | Second assessment and targeted review | Explain why answers are right/wrong |
| 21 | Light revision, logistics and rest | No major new deployments |

30 November: exam day.

Suggested daily routine: 45–60 min learning, 60–90 min hands-on/scenarios, 30 min practice questions, 10 min notes. If time is limited, prioritize official objectives and assessment review over optional deployments.
